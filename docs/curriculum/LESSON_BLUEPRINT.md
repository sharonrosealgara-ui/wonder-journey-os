# WONDER JOURNEY LESSON BLUEPRINT

## Core Requirements
Every lesson must include:
- Adventure title & scheduled date
- Unit and monthly theme
- Mixed-age target
- Learning objectives & essential question
- Factual background & vocabulary
- Tagalog / Hiligaynon components
- English component
- Subject connection (geography, science, math, history, culture, cooking, art, music, Christian character)
- Materials & real-media requirements
- Teacher preparation notes
- Answer key & reflection
- "What I learned today" & gratitude prompt
- Family challenge & progress badge
- Factual sources and media attribution notes

## Mixed-Age Design
### Beginner Support
- Simpler vocabulary, visual matching, tracing/pointing, reduced writing.

### Core Activity
- Standard objective, class discussion, interactive game, individual/team task.

### Advanced Challenge
- Deeper research, extended writing, analysis/presentation.

## Standard Timing (60 minutes)
- Welcome/Prayer/Gratitude: 5 min
- Adventure Intro: 5 min
- Main Visual Story: 12 min
- Core Activity: 10 min
- Interactive Game: 10 min
- Hands-on Activity: 10 min
- Quiz & Reflection: 5 min
- Family Challenge & Closing: 3 min
