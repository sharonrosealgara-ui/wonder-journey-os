# TEACHER PREPARATION GUIDE

## Teacher Portal Visibility Matrix
**Teacher-Only Elements:**
- Presenter timer
- Private notes & preparation checklist
- Answer keys
- Attendance & annotation controls
- Lesson editing & media attribution administration
- Internal fact-check notes

## Preparation Steps
1. Review the schedule for the week.
2. Confirm legal/family holidays and travel dates.
3. Review factual claims (Fact Check Register).
4. Gather materials listed in the lesson blueprint.
