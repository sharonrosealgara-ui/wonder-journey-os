# FAMILY CHALLENGE GUIDE

## Purpose
To connect the lesson content to daily family life and encourage offline interaction.

## Family Portal Visibility Matrix
**Family-Visible Elements:**
- Lesson title
- Approved child-facing instructions
- Factual media
- Activities & quizzes (without answer keys)
- Progress celebrations
- Family challenges & learner-safe reflection prompts

**NEVER EXPOSE:**
- Answer keys, private notes, teacher timers, internal comments, personal family records, or admin controls.
